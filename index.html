Legal Document Classification

Fine-tuning ModernBERT to route long legal documents into 30 categories
My role
Individual entry, Kaggle competition
What I built
Fully fine-tuned ModernBERT-base on 50,840 documents and scored 12,710 test documents. Long text was handled with regex cleaning and head-tail truncation (128 head + 384 tail tokens). Class imbalance was countered with inverse-frequency weighted cross-entropy and a stratified split. Training used a cosine LR schedule, warmup, gradient accumulation and mixed precision, reaching 71.9% validation accuracy and 0.56 macro-F1.

Python PyTorch Hugging Face ModernBERT Scikit-learn

Purchase Value Prediction

Predicting revenue per web session, from clicks to conversions
My role
Individual entry, Kaggle competition
What I built
A regression pipeline for a heavily right-skewed target (most sessions have no purchase) on 116K sessions and 52 columns. I cleaned placeholder values and removed 5,304 duplicates, built a Scikit-learn Pipeline, and selected 25 features with RFE and XGBoost. After benchmarking Gradient Boosting (R² 0.25), Random Forest (0.40) and XGBoost (0.47) and diagnosing overfitting, 5-fold GridSearchCV lifted R² to 0.52.
